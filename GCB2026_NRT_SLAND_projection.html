Current GCB projection

For GCB2025, current-year SLAND was projected as the residual of the other projected carbon-budget components:

\[ S_{LAND}=E_{FOS}+E_{LUC}-G_{ATM}-S_{OCEAN} \]

Question: can near-real-time DGVM simulations provide an additional constraint?

Global Carbon Budget 2025, Sect. 2.6.2
https://doi.org/10.5194/essd-18-3211-2026

Two NRT DGVMs are now available

Two DGVM systems are available with near-real-time meteorology + seasonal forecast:

JULES and ORCHIDEE

The proposed predictor is the year-to-year change, not absolute SLAND:

\[ \Delta S_{LAND,t}=S_{LAND,t}-S_{LAND,t-1} \]

The two models show similar historical IAV

Model 2026 SLAND 2025→2026 change
JULES 2.556 0.924
ORCHIDEE 0.578 -0.239

The two-model mean change is 0.343 GtC yr⁻¹.

JULES and ORCHIDEE disagree on the sign of the 2026 change, so the model spread should remain explicit.

Does NRT ΔSLAND predict GCB ΔSLAND?

The two-model NRT mean annual change is strongly related to the annual change of the GCB DGVM ensemble.

  • r = 0.88
  • R² = 0.77
  • RMSE = 0.48 GtC yr⁻¹

\[ \Delta S_{LAND}^{GCB} = 0.048 + 0.714 \Delta S_{LAND}^{NRT} \]

2026 predicted change

Two-model NRT mean:

\[ \Delta S_{LAND,2026}^{NRT} = 0.343\ \mathrm{GtC\,yr^{-1}} \]

Calibrated GCB change:

\[ \widehat{\Delta S}_{LAND,2026}^{GCB} = 0.293\ \mathrm{GtC\,yr^{-1}} \]

Absolute 2026 estimate depends on the baseline

2025 baseline 2025 SLAND 2026 estimate 95% PI lower 95% PI upper
GCB S2 (1700 land cover) 4.43 4.72 3.74 5.71
New GCB SLAND (S3-S4+S0) 3.55 3.85 2.87 4.83

Historical GCB S2 uses 1700 land cover; the NRT runs use 2010 land cover.

This year GCB also has a new SLAND definition:

\[ S_{LAND}^{new}=S3-S4+S0 \]

A practical approach is therefore:

use the NRT DGVMs to estimate ΔSLAND, then apply that change to the preferred 2025 GCB SLAND baseline.

The 2026 NRT mean suggests a modest increase, but the two models disagree on its sign.

Note, dL not included here yet.

Does the prediction work?

As a simple hindcast, fit the NRT–GCB relationship using data only up to 2024, then ask what the JULES+ORCHIDEE 2024→2025 change would have predicted for the 2025 land sink.

Two model NRT mean (change in sink from 2024 to 2025): \[ \Delta S_{LAND,2025}^{NRT} = 2.166\ \mathrm{GtC\,yr^{-1}} \]

The NRT method predicts a 2025 land sink of 3.75 GtC yr⁻¹, compared with the realised GCB value of 3.55 GtC yr⁻¹.

Prediction interval: 2.74–4.77 GtC yr⁻¹.

So, starting from the 2024 GCB land sink, the NRT DGVM change successfully captures most of the large increase in SLAND observed in 2025.
